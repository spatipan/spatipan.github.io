01

Nature Sci Rep · 2025

AI-based emergency triage

A machine-learning model predicting ICU admission from the emergency department, developed and internally validated on 163,452 ED visits. XGBoost reached AUROC 0.917 (vs. 0.882 for the CTAS triage scale), nearly doubling precision-recall (AUPRC 0.629 vs. 0.333) by reading free-text Thai chief complaints with multilingual sentence embeddings. Reported to the TRIPOD+AI standard.

03 / Working approach

One practice,
three disciplines.

Clinical judgement defines the problem. Research methods test the claim. Engineering makes the result useful.

  1. DefineClarify the clinical need, users, workflow, evidence, and meaningful outcome.
  2. ValidateChoose methods that can support the claim—not merely produce an impressive metric.
  3. TranslateDesign for responsible use in real clinical or research environments.
